Who this website is for

This website presents Cankaj Super Car Wash and BC Stone Mason & Construction Restoration, independent services operated by Bledar Cankaj in County Clare. Questions about this notice can be sent to bledar24@gmail.com.

Enquiries

The enquiry form prepares a WhatsApp message on your device. The website itself does not store the details entered into that form. If you contact the business through WhatsApp, telephone or email, the information you send is used to respond to your enquiry and manage any work you request.

Feedback

The private-feedback form prepares an email on your device. It does not submit information to or store information on this website. Public reviews are handled by Google under Google’s own privacy terms.

Maps and external services

The car-wash page embeds Google Maps and links to Google Maps, WhatsApp and email services. These services may process technical information or set cookies under their own policies when loaded or opened.

Analytics and advertising

This version of the website does not use advertising trackers or a separate analytics service. If analytics or an online form provider is added later, this notice and any required consent controls should be updated first.

Your choices

You can avoid the embedded map and external messaging services by contacting Bledar directly on 087 707 0331. You may also ask about information previously supplied in a direct enquiry.
