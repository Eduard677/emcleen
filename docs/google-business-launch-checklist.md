# Cankaj Google Business launch checklist

The strongest local-search opportunity is the existing Cankaj Super Car Wash listing. As checked in September 2026, it shows 4.7 from 14 reviews, no website and the option to claim the business.

## Bledar needs to complete

1. Open the [existing Google Maps listing](https://maps.google.com/?cid=18333088850512823541). Do not create a duplicate listing.
2. Select **Claim this business** and complete Google's owner-verification process.
3. Add `https://cankaj-dual-services-demo.vercel.app/auto` as the website until a permanent domain is connected.
4. Confirm the displayed opening hours: Monday–Saturday 09:00–18:00 and Sunday closed.
5. Confirm the business address and pin at Halpins Service Station, Limerick Road, Newmarket-on-Fergus, V95 YR25.
6. Add the business logo, a clear forecourt photograph and at least ten recent photographs of real work.
7. Reply calmly to every new review, including critical reviews. Never argue or reveal customer information.
8. Ask customers for honest reviews using the website's `/review` page. Do not offer incentives or filter customers by expected rating.
