FAQ

Questions.

Where are you based?

Ennis, Co. Clare. Drop the car to me, park up, and either collect it when it's done or wait the hour.

What's the difference between the Maintenance Detail and a one-off detail?

The Maintenance Detail keeps an already-clean car clean: exterior wash and dry, interior hoover, tyre shine, done in under an hour. The Detail and Extensive Detail are one-off deep cleans that reset the car — the Detail goes deeper inside and out, and the Extensive Detail is a complete interior and exterior job. Most people start with one of those and then keep it there with the weekly Maintenance Detail.

How long does it take?

The Maintenance Detail is under an hour. The Detail and Extensive Detail take longer — you'll get a time when you book.

How does the weekly subscription work?

You get a set slot at the same time each week for €30. Manage it online — pause, reschedule or cancel any time.

What if it rains?

It's a covered setup, so the weather doesn't stop your slot.

Can I pause or cancel?

Yes, any time, no notice period.

What if my car is badly soiled — pet hair, smoke, spills?

All doable, but priced separately since it's more work. Send a photo on WhatsApp and I'll quote it.

How do I pay?

Cash, card, Revolut, or Stripe.

Do you detail all cars?

Any car, any condition.
