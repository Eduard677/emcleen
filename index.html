FAQ

Questions.

Where are you based?

Ennis, Co. Clare. Drop the car to me, park up, and either collect it when it's done or wait the hour.

Do you have slots free?

There are only so many weekly slots, and they go first come, first served. Text me and I'll tell you what's open — if your preferred time is taken I'll offer you the nearest one.

What's the difference between the Maintenance Detail and a one-off detail?

The Maintenance Detail keeps an already-clean car clean: snow foam pre-wash, two-bucket contact wash, wheels and tyres, interior vacuum, dash and plastics wiped, hand dried — about an hour. The Detail adds the work a weekly wash doesn't reach: wheel arches and door shuts, a deep interior clean down to the trim, seat rails and seat belts, mats deep cleaned, the boot detailed and the glass polished. The Extensive Detail goes further again on the paint — clay bar decontamination, tar removal, and a wax or sealant, with the alloys done including the barrels and every interior surface detailed and dressed. Most people start with a Detail or Extensive Detail and then hold it there with the weekly Maintenance Detail.

How long does it take?

The Maintenance Detail is about an hour. The Detail and Extensive Detail take longer — you'll get a time when you book.

How does the weekly subscription work?

You get a set slot at the same time each week for €30. Text me any time to pause, move or cancel your slot.

What if it rains?

It's a covered setup, so the weather doesn't stop your slot.

Can I pause or cancel?

Yes, any time, no notice period.

What if my car is badly soiled — pet hair, smoke, spills?

All doable, but priced separately since it's more work. Send a photo on WhatsApp and I'll quote it.

How do I pay?

Cash, card, Revolut, or Stripe.

Do you detail all cars?

Any car, any condition.
