FAQ

Questions.

Where are you based?

Ennis, Co. Clare. Drop the car to me, park up, and either collect it when it's done or wait the hour.

What's the difference between the weekly wash and a detail?

The weekly maintenance wash keeps an already-clean car clean: exterior wash and dry, interior hoover, tyre shine, done in under an hour. A detail is a one-off deep clean that resets the car — the mini detail goes deeper inside and out, and the full detail is a complete interior and exterior job. Most people start with a detail and then keep it there with the weekly wash.

How long does it take?

The weekly wash is under an hour. Details take longer — you'll get a time when you book.

How does the weekly subscription work?

You get a set slot at the same time each week for €30. Manage it online — pause, reschedule or cancel any time.

What if it rains?

It's a covered setup, so the weather doesn't stop your slot.

Can I pause or cancel?

Yes, any time, no notice period.

What if my car is badly soiled — pet hair, smoke, spills?

All doable, but priced separately since it's more work. Send a photo on WhatsApp and I'll quote it.

How do I pay?

Cash, card, Revolut, or Stripe.

Do you detail all cars?

Any car, any condition.
