FAQ

Questions.

Where are you based?

Ennis, Co. Clare.

What's the difference between the weekly wash and a detail?

The weekly maintenance wash keeps a clean car clean — exterior wash and dry, interior hoover, tyre shine, done in about 40 minutes. A detail is a one-off deep clean that resets the car: the mini detail goes deeper inside and out, and the full detail is a complete interior and exterior restoration.

How long does it take?

The weekly wash takes about 40 minutes. Details take longer — we'll give you a time when you book.

How does the weekly subscription work?

You get a set slot at the same time every week. Drop the car, collect it 40 minutes later, €30 a week. Text us to pause or change your slot any time.

Do you detail all cars?

Yes — any car, any condition.
